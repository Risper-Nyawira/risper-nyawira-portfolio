# Citation, licence and data provenance

## Primary dataset

Winowiecki, L. A., Thiongo Maina, J., Bargués-Tobella, A., Otieno Onyango, J., Dennis, N., Kersting, D., & Vågen, T.-G. (2022). *Spatial Assessments of Changes in Soil Health Indicators in Kenya* (Version 2.1). World Agroforestry (ICRAF). https://doi.org/10.34725/DVN/MR2COM

The source page identifies `table_plotproperties_Kenya_cleaned_dataversecsv.tab` as a 131-variable, 316-observation table and lists a companion `ldsf_description of codesv5.tab` file.

## Licence

The dataset record lists a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

When reusing the dataset, provide appropriate attribution, link to the licence, and state that your working subset and derived summary tables are modifications. Do not imply that ICRAF or the authors endorse this analysis.

## Project data policy

The raw source data are not copied into this repository. Obtain the official source files from the DOI and apply the documented Sichei filter locally. This repository records methods and preliminary summary findings rather than providing a replacement for the source.

## Reproducibility note

All counts and percentages in these notes describe the current prepared Sichei subset of 160 rows. Verify the working workbook and source codes before relying on or externally publishing results.
