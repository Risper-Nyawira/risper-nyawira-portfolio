# Ideas Bank: Regenerative Agriculture, Food Systems & Agricultural Innovation

This document records four ideas I am exploring across agricultural data, farmer services, regenerative farming, and high-value crop production.

**Important:** These are concepts at different stages of development. Listing an idea here does not mean it has been implemented, field-tested, commercially validated, or funded. The purpose of this register is to document the direction, identify assumptions, and plan practical next steps.

## 1. RegeneraSense

**Stage:** Concept development; pitch deck prepared.

### Concept
RegeneraSense is a proposed data-driven regenerative agriculture intelligence platform for water-stressed regions. It would help farmers, practitioners, and other agricultural stakeholders make better-informed decisions about regenerative practices and monitor outcomes.

### Potential focus areas
- Soil health and regeneration
- Water-use efficiency and water security
- Biodiversity and ecosystem resilience
- Climate adaptation and, where measurement is feasible, carbon outcomes
- Farm productivity, food security, and livelihoods
- Monitoring, evaluation, and learning (MEL/MEAL)

### Key assumptions to investigate
- Which users have the most urgent decision-making problem?
- What data can be collected reliably and affordably?
- Which indicators can demonstrate meaningful change?
- What would make the platform useful in a specific farming context?

### Next milestone
Prepare an evidence-based research brief and define one narrow use case for a demonstrator dashboard or prototype.

## 2. Afya Verified

**Stage:** Integrated platform concept.

### Concept
Afya Verified is a proposed farmer-support platform that combines market access, agricultural research and extension, and production-practice verification. It brings together three connected functions rather than treating them as separate products.

### Proposed components

#### A. Farmer-to-buyer market access
- Help farmers discover potential buyers and market opportunities.
- Support the sharing of relevant produce, volume, quality, and availability information.
- Explore ways to improve transparency around market requirements and transactions.

#### B. Research and extension services
- Connect farmers with practical, evidence-informed agricultural guidance.
- Help agricultural officers and extension providers share relevant recommendations.
- Make useful information on production, pest management, soil health, and climate risks easier to access.

#### C. Production verification and traceability
- Explore records for production practices and chemical use.
- Support credible verification of production claims, including organic claims where applicable.
- Help farmers understand the documentation and standards buyers require.

### Potential future capability
Decision-support insights on production, market trends, and climate conditions could be explored after the core user need is established. Any AI-supported recommendations would need reliable data and appropriate expert review.

### Key assumptions to investigate
- Which farmers and buyers would use the first version?
- What trust, verification, and data-quality safeguards are needed?
- Which services already exist, and what gap would Afya Verified address?
- How could the platform remain accessible to farmers with limited connectivity or digital skills?

### Next milestone
Map the user journey and define a minimum viable product (MVP) with one priority user group and one testable use case.

## 3. Kabukuru Farm

**Stage:** Farm concept development.

### Concept
Kabukuru Farm is a proposed integrated regenerative farming model that brings together crop production, trees and agroforestry, livestock, and circular use of farm resources.

### Potential components
- Diversified crop and tree systems
- Livestock integrated with crop production where practical
- Manure management, composting, and soil-fertility improvement
- Water conservation and efficient use of farm inputs
- Agroforestry and biodiversity
- Exploration of carbon-market opportunities only if eligibility, measurement, costs, and commercial feasibility can be demonstrated

### Key assumptions to investigate
- What land, water, infrastructure, labour, and starting capital are available?
- Which enterprises suit the local agroecological conditions?
- How can the farm balance ecological benefits with reliable income?
- Which activities should be introduced first, and which depend on later investment?

### Next milestone
Develop a phased farm master plan covering land use, enterprise selection, resource flows, costs, risks, and potential revenue streams.

## 4. Regenerative Herbs and Spices Farm — Limuru

**Stage:** Early planning.

### Concept
A proposed two-acre herbs and spices farm in Limuru, exploring high-value crops and regenerative production practices. Crops previously considered include vanilla, cardamom, cinnamon, cumin, and curry leaves; these are options to investigate, not a final planting list.

### Areas to assess
- Local climate, altitude, soil, water, and crop suitability
- Crop-specific growing conditions, time to maturity, and input requirements
- Availability and cost of quality planting material
- Buyer demand, quality standards, pricing, and routes to market
- Labour, infrastructure, post-harvest handling, and storage
- Intercropping, soil cover, composting, and other suitable regenerative practices
- Financial viability and the risks of relying on a small number of crops

### Key assumption to investigate
The most attractive crop on paper may not be the most suitable or profitable crop for the site. Crop selection should follow agronomic and market research.

### Next milestone
Prepare a crop-feasibility comparison and shortlist crops only after checking growing requirements, planting-material availability, and credible market opportunities.

## How the ideas relate

| Idea | Primary role | Possible connection |
|---|---|---|
| RegeneraSense | Agricultural data and regenerative-practice decision support | Could develop monitoring approaches relevant to regenerative farms |
| Afya Verified | Farmer services, market access, extension, and verification | Could help farmers access information and understand buyer requirements |
| Kabukuru Farm | Integrated regenerative farm model | Could serve as a practical learning context if developed |
| Limuru Herbs and Spices Farm | Focused high-value crop enterprise | Could test crop economics and suitable regenerative practices at farm scale |

These ideas share interests in evidence-based decisions, farmer livelihoods, sustainable production, and better agricultural markets. They remain distinct concepts and should be evaluated on their own feasibility.

## Suggested development sequence

1. **Research before investment:** Gather evidence, identify users or customers, and document the most important unknowns.
2. **Validate the problem:** Speak with relevant farmers, buyers, extension officers, researchers, and other stakeholders where access is possible.
3. **Build a small demonstrator:** For data or platform ideas, start with a simple workflow, dataset, mock-up, or dashboard rather than a full product.
4. **Test feasibility:** For farm ideas, assess agronomy, markets, costs, water, labour, and risks before committing resources.
5. **Record evidence honestly:** Distinguish desk research, stakeholder feedback, prototypes, pilots, and validated results.
