01 Research portfolio

Kenya Agricultural Policy Impact Tracker

Are agricultural interventions delivering meaningful outcomes?

A structured assessment of ten Kenyan agricultural interventions, examining the evidence available on implementation, productivity, farmer livelihoods, markets, food security and sustainability.

Approach A reusable 18-step assessment framework, intervention summaries, evidence register and integrated research workbook.

Evidence standard Separates programme-reported results, observed outcomes and credible causal impact evidence.

This is a preliminary evidence-tracking exercise, not an official programme evaluation or ten completed impact evaluations.

  • Agricultural policy
  • Evidence assessment
  • Excel
  • Research synthesis

03

Kenya Agricultural Market Intelligence

From agricultural price data to market intelligence.

A tomato-market pilot combining market-price records with production and climate context to examine price differences, seasonality and variability across markets.

Workflow Source inventory, data audits, preparation scripts, SQL/SQLite and analytical outputs.

Market-point climate measures are contextual proxies, not proof of the conditions in the areas supplying each market.

04

Kenya Food Security Analysis

How do production, climate and markets relate to food security?

An analytical workflow linking maize production, climate variability, market dynamics and a national food-insecurity indicator, with documented data preparation and analysis.

Workflow Cleaned data, SQLite views, Python notebook, charts and a Power BI dashboard.

Limitation The food-insecurity series has eight observations and uses a three-year moving average; the analysis is descriptive, not causal.

  • SQL / SQLite
  • Python
  • Power BI
  • Food security

05

Sichei Soil Health · exploratory analysis

What do plot records show about land use and recorded erosion?

An exploratory analysis of 160 Sichei-labelled plot records, documenting data checks, land-use and management summaries, a selected data dictionary and interpretation limits.

Research discipline The analysis is descriptive; the limited positive erosion observations do not support robust group inference.

  • Soil & land health
