01 Active / developing

Kenya Agricultural Market Intelligence

From price data to practical market intelligence.

Using tomato as its first value chain, this reproducible project integrates market prices, national production context, climate proxies and spatial context to examine price differences, seasonality and volatility across Kenyan markets.

Coverage 783 cleaned price records across eight markets, with no missing required fields.

Observed pattern Kisii and Mombasa had the highest average wholesale prices in the selected panel; Embu was the most variable.

The work documents coverage and uncertainty, distinguishing observed associations from causal claims.

  • Python
  • SQL / SQLite
  • Market analysis
  • Climate context
  • Spatial data

02

Food-producing communities & food security

Why does producing food not always guarantee food security?

An analysis of maize production, yield, prices, cultivated area, climate indicators and food insecurity—read as evidence of patterns, not proof of causality.

Key finding Food insecurity rose from 50.7% to 72.8% across the available rolling periods, despite continued national maize production.

  • SQL
  • Python
  • Power BI

Kenya Food Security Analysis

Production, climate and markets in food-security outcomes.

A complete analytical workflow from source data through cleaned datasets, SQLite and SQL views to Python analysis, charts and Power BI-oriented outputs.
